AKC Grand Champion Eynhallow Ladybird for Hound Ridge's Scottish deerhound litter is now nine weeks old. 

Here are just a few of the lovely and consistent, large litter whelped October 24 from Fritzen’s Doc of the Bay for Hound Ridge X GCH Eynhallow Ladybird for Hound Ridge.
